How the KPI arrows work

Every customer has a join date in 2015. Each card compares the full base with the base as it stood at the end of the month before the latest join month (end of November).

  • Join date = DATEPARSE('dd.MMM.yy', [Date Joined])
  • Latest month start = DATETRUNC('month', {MAX([Join date])})
  • Prior avg balance = AVG(IF [Join date] < [Latest month start] THEN [Balance] END)
  • The arrow and colour follow the sign of the change (green up, red down) and update when the page is filtered.

Design

  • Dark theme: page #0B1120, cards #151E2E with rounded corners.
  • One accent for data: cyan #22D3EE bars, amber #F59E0B for the second gender.
  • Question-style titles, sorted bars and data labels on every chart. Age and balance band widths can be changed with sliders.

Sample dataset of 4,014 UK retail bank customers with customer ID, gender, age, region, job classification, join date and balance. Names and IDs are fictional. The data travels inside the packaged workbook.
